# Calculator specifications — draft

## Salary
Inputs: gross/net target, period, employment and tax status, eligible components. Outputs: take-home pay, itemized tax and contributions, assumptions. Rules must be sourced and versioned; no hard-coded rates without verified references.

## Loan
Inputs: principal, nominal annual rate, tenor, interest method. Outputs: installment, interest total, principal total, amortization schedule. Specify effective vs flat and rounding policy.

## Savings
Inputs: initial savings, recurring contribution, annual rate assumption, compounding frequency, target/date. Outputs: ending value, contributions, growth, time to target.

## Investment
Inputs: initial amount, periodic investment, assumed return, period, scenarios. Outputs: projection and contribution vs modeled growth. Returns are illustrative, not guaranteed; consider fees/tax/inflation as future enhancements.

## Cross-cutting
IDR formatting, transparent formulas, decimal precision, validation, empty states, references, mobile accessibility and deterministic fixtures.
